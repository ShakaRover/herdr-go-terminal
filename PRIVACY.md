# HerdrGoT Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2, 2026

HerdrGoT is a mobile client for connecting to coding agents that run on a host controlled by the user. Chengdu Yisitai Technology Co., Ltd ("we", "us", or "our") does not operate a HerdrGoT cloud service and does not collect personal data through the HerdrGoT app.

## Data processed by the app

To provide its features, HerdrGoT processes the following information on your device:

- Connection details for the herdrgot-bridge instances you add, including the bridge address, network secret, display label, and any custom request header you configure.
- Agent status, terminal output, session history, workspace information, and other data returned by your own herdrgot-bridge.
- Replies, commands, key presses, and images that you choose to send to your own host.
- Local preferences and notification state.

This information is used only to connect the app to hosts you choose and to provide the features you request. It is not sent to us.

## Storage and retention

Connection details and preferences are stored locally on your device until you remove them or uninstall the app. Agent data remains subject to the storage and retention settings of the host and tools you control. We do not retain a copy on our servers.

## Network transport

HerdrGoT can use an EasyTier virtual network to connect to your herdrgot-bridge. The network secret is a credential and should be kept private. EasyTier relays may carry encrypted network traffic when a direct peer-to-peer connection is unavailable, but they cannot read the encrypted application traffic. Network operators may necessarily process limited routing metadata, such as IP addresses and connection timing, to deliver traffic.

## Photos and images

If you choose an image from your photo library, HerdrGoT sends that image only to the host you selected. HerdrGoT does not upload it to us. The app does not access photos unless you initiate the image-selection action.

## Notifications and background activity

HerdrGoT may periodically contact your selected host in the background and generate local notifications when an agent needs attention. Notification content is produced and stored on your device.

## Analytics, advertising, and tracking

HerdrGoT does not include advertising SDKs, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking. We do not sell personal data.

## Third-party services

Hosts, coding-agent tools, network services, and other software you choose to use with HerdrGoT are governed by their own terms and privacy practices. Apps downloaded through the App Store are also subject to Apple's privacy practices.

## Children

HerdrGoT is a developer tool and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Security

You are responsible for securing your host, network secret, and pairing QR code. Do not share the network secret or pairing QR code with anyone who should not have access to your agents.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as HerdrGoT changes. The effective date above will be updated when we make material changes.
